SightMooch Support

SightMooch helps you discover the stories behind the sights you pass as you explore the city.

Need help or spotted a mistake? Email gtitechv@gmail.com and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

Do I need an account?

No. SightMooch works straight away, with no sign-up and no personal details needed.

Where are my favourites and visited places saved?

They're saved on your phone only. If you delete the app, they're deleted too. Your phone's own backup may restore them if you move to a new phone, but we can't recover them for you, because we never see them.

Why do the distances look wrong?

Check that SightMooch is allowed to use your location in your phone's settings. With no signal, for example on the Underground, distances are measured from your last known location until you're back above ground. If location is turned off, distances are measured from central London.

Can I use SightMooch offline?

Yes. The list of sights and their descriptions work without a connection. The map needs a connection to load, so use the list view when you're offline.

Some information about a sight is wrong or missing

Thank you for letting us know. Please email us with the name of the sight and what needs changing, and we'll update it. Sight information is updated without needing a new version of the app.
