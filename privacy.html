SightMooch Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

In short: SightMooch does not ask you to create an account and does not collect, store or share your personal data. Your favourites, visited places and location stay on your device.

Who we are

SightMooch is a sightseeing app developed by Ian Guest, based in the United Kingdom. If you have any questions about this policy, please contact gtitechv@gmail.com.

Information we do not collect

SightMooch does not require an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, contact details or any other personal information. The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking tools.

Your location

If you allow it, SightMooch uses your device's location to show how far away each sight is and to sort sights by distance. Your location is only used within the app on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. The app saves your last known location on your device, so distances still work when you have no signal, for example on the Underground.

You can turn location access off at any time in your device settings. The app will still work, but distances will be measured from central London.

Favourites and visited places

Sights you save as favourites or mark as visited are stored only on your device. We cannot see them, and they are deleted if you delete the app.

Content the app downloads

To show up-to-date information, SightMooch downloads sight information, photos and maps from the following services. As with any website or app, these services receive standard technical information needed to deliver content to your device, such as your IP address. They do not receive your location from SightMooch.

  • Google Sheets, which hosts the list of sights (Google Privacy Policy).
  • GitHub Pages, which hosts photos and this page (GitHub Privacy Statement).
  • Mapbox, which provides the map (Mapbox Privacy Policy).

Links to other apps and websites

Some features open other apps or websites, such as walking directions in Google Maps. Once you leave SightMooch, the privacy policy of that app or website applies.

Children

SightMooch does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "last updated" date.
